Data privacy

Accounts, quiz records, learning progress and results are handled under the principles of the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).

  • Sign-in is limited to school emails (@cscqc.edu.ph).
  • Stored personal data: name, school email, student code, grade and quiz results.
  • Only admins can create, approve or revoke staff access.
